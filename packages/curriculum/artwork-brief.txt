# The artwork, as data. One line per asset, pipe-delimited:
#
#   kind | id | what to draw | must NOT read as | note
#
# kind is one of:
#   word      a Level 1 word, shown in Robot Talk and Picture Match
#   mnemonic  a letter's memory hook, shown on the Meet card and as a hint
#   start     one of a letter's start pictures, shown in Picture Start
#   creature  a Soundling, earned when its sound is mastered
#   cast      a recurring character
#
# scripts/generate-artwork-brief.ts joins this to the curriculum and writes
# docs/ARTWORK-BRIEF.md. It FAILS if a line names an asset the curriculum does
# not have, or if a curriculum picture has no line here — so the brief and the
# game cannot drift apart. Planned additions and retirements are listed in the
# two blocks at the end and are reported as a delta rather than as an error.
#
# "must NOT read as" is the whole job. Every one of these is a word a child
# must NAME from the picture with nobody prompting them, and seven of the nine
# shipped placeholders fail that: a child shown the current `pat` says "dog".
#
# --- the words ---------------------------------------------------------------
# Verbs and adjectives are the hard ones. An object drawn alone becomes its own
# name — a chair is "chair", never "sit" — so the ACTION has to be the subject
# of the picture and the object incidental.
word|map|An opened paper map, creased, with a coastline and a dotted route. Held flat, filling the frame.|a globe; a phone; a treasure chest|The one shipped picture that already works. Keep its reading, change only the style.
word|tap|A garden tap, side on, with a single stream of water running from it.|a hose; a sink; a bath|Australian: a tap, not a faucet. The water stream is what stops it reading as a pipe.
word|mat|A woven doormat lying flat on the ground, from slightly above, with a visible weave and fringe.|a rug in a room; a yoga mat; a person|The shipped picture is a meditating person and a child says "person". Draw the object, empty, with nothing on it.
word|pat|A hand mid-pat on a dog's head, the hand large and nearest the viewer, the dog small and only partly in frame.|a dog|HIGHEST RISK ON THE SHEET. The shipped picture is a whole dog and every child says "dog". If the hand cannot be made to dominate, say so and we will drop the word rather than ship a picture that fails.
word|sit|Sam sitting squarely on a low stool, front on, knees bent, hands on knees.|a chair; a stool; standing|Sam is the subject and the stool is a minimal shape under them. Contrast hard with `sip` below: this one is NOT holding anything.
word|sip|Pam drinking through a straw, cup at the mouth, cheeks drawn in, eyes closed.|a cup; a drink; juice|The straw at the lips is the whole picture. Must not be mistakable for `sit` at a glance — different character, standing, cup held high.
word|pit|A dug pit in sandy ground, deep enough to show its far wall, a spade standing in the heap beside it.|a hole in a wall; a cave; a tunnel|RISK: a child may say "hole". The spade and the heap of sand are what make it a dug pit. Test this one first.
word|sam|Sam, head and shoulders, waving, smiling, front on.|a generic child|Only works once a child knows Sam. See The cast below.
word|pam|Pam, head and shoulders, waving, smiling, front on.|a generic child; Sam|Must be unmistakable from Sam at 56px: different hair silhouette and different colour, not just a different face.
# --- the letters: mnemonics --------------------------------------------------
# Shown large on the Meet card, the first time a child ever sees the letter, and
# again as the first hint when they are stuck. It is also the creature's animal,
# so these and the creatures below must look like the same world.
mnemonic|s|A snake curled into the shape of the letter s, head at the top.|a worm; a rope|The letterform IS the mnemonic here. The curve should be readable as `s` without the letter beside it.
mnemonic|a|A single red apple with a stalk and one leaf.|a tomato; a cherry|Plain and frontal. This is the vowel a child will meet second, so it carries a lot.
mnemonic|t|A teddy bear sitting, arms out, front on.|a dog; a generic bear|Jointed arms and a stitched muzzle read as "teddy"; a realistic bear reads as "bear".
mnemonic|p|A pig, front on, snout prominent, ears forward.|a dog; a cow|
mnemonic|i|An insect — a plump caterpillar on a leaf, side on, with distinct segments.|a worm; a snake|Caterpillar rather than beetle: it must not be confused with Sisi the snake, and a child reliably says "caterpillar"... which is NOT "insect". Discuss before drawing: the mnemonic word may need to change with the picture.
mnemonic|m|A crescent moon with a calm face, three-quarter.|a banana; a star|
mnemonic|f|A fish, side on, one visible eye, fanned tail.|a whale; a shark|
